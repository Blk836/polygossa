A Rainy Saturday
category: daily-life
On Saturday morning, Sam wakes up late. Rain is falling on the window, and the street outside is quiet. He makes a cup of tea and sits on the sofa with his cat, Pepper.

Sam wanted to go to the park with his friends today, but the weather is too bad. So he calls his sister instead. She lives in another city, and they don't talk very often. They laugh about old family holidays at the beach.

In the afternoon, the rain stops. Sam puts on his boots and walks to the little bakery at the end of the street. He buys fresh bread and two cakes, one for him and one for his neighbour, Mrs Patel. She is eighty years old and loves chocolate.

When he gets home, the sun is shining. "It's not a bad Saturday after all," Sam thinks.
